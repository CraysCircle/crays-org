Crays Mercedes Island

Island resort in the Philippines

The Crays Mercedes Island is the first physical Crays Club Resort rollout: a Bitcoin-native hospitality, work and community destination with a private airstrip, beaches, villas and legacy resort infrastructure.

The island resort on Apuao Grande Island in the Philippines is the first large-scale community resort rollout under the Crays brand, operated by Amihan, the owners of the 64-hectare island, who independently lead the project planning, development and operations as members of the Crays Association under a Crays brand DAO license.
