Nostr basics

What exactly is Nostr?

Nostr is an open protocol for social identity and signed messages. You can think of it as a shared language that apps and servers can use. Your identity is based on a key pair, not a username owned by one platform.

Is Nostr a blockchain, token or company?

No. Nostr is not a blockchain and it does not require its own token. Bitcoin and Lightning can connect to it for payments, but Nostr itself is about identity, messages, apps and independent servers.

Why should you care if you are not technical?

Because it changes who owns your social identity. On normal platforms, your account, audience and visibility can be changed by the platform. With Nostr, your identity can move across clients and services.

What are apps and servers in plain language?

An app is what you open on your phone or browser. Independent servers pass Nostr activity between apps. You can use different apps and different servers without rebuilding your identity from zero.
