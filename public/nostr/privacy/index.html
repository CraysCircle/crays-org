Start where risk enters

Protect the key before the feature becomes attractive.

Privacy is not a mood. It is a sequence of product decisions: where signing happens, what becomes public, what can be encrypted, what a relay can observe and how trust becomes visible without giving away control.

Identity begins with the keyPublic keys, private keys, npub, nsec and signatures become readable before app choice. Security advice without panicKeep private keys out of websites, explain public events and turn risk into clear habits. Threat models make trust honestThe deep dive shows what can break and where product design reduces the damage. Remote signing changes the roomNIP-46 is useful when signing needs to move away from the website but stay usable. Encryption has limitsEncrypted payloads matter most when the remaining metadata surface stays visible. Auth should prove without oversharingHTTP auth belongs here when access depends on signatures and request context. Trust signals need careSearch, reputation and web-of-trust only help when they avoid turning identity into noise. Freedom needs a safety layerCensorship resistance is stronger when moderation and abuse pressure are explained plainly.

Safety layer

Move from identity to trust without losing the thread.

Privacy works best when it feels calm. The path moves from keys to signers, from public data to encryption, from abuse pressure to web-of-trust and from attractive features to responsible product boundaries.

01Start with identityKeys and identity explain the mental model behind npub, nsec and signatures. 02Make the safe habit visibleSecurity becomes practical when private material never gets pasted or exposed. 03Move signing out of the danger zoneRemote signing explains how convenience and key control can coexist. 04Explain encryption honestlyEncryption pages keep the promise honest by showing the remaining metadata surface. 05Read trust as a public signalWeb-of-trust connects reputation, labels and discovery without pretending trust is automatic. 06Open every safety pageCompliance, abuse, censorship and standards references stay within reach.
