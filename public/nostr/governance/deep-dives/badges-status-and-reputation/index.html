Why this topic exists

The internet already has social networks, messaging apps, publishing tools and payment products. Nostr matters here because it lets builders separate identity from a single operator. In the case of badges, status and reputation, the relevant question is how open keys, signed events, relays and client choice change the product assumptions.

The topic is not useful as a slogan. It is useful when a reader can connect issuer trust, earned status, bought status and profile display to a real user journey: create an identity, choose a client, publish or authorize an event, route it through relays, and make it visible to the right people or services.
