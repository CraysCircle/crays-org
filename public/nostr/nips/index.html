Start with the product question

Read the standard through the thing it makes possible.

A standards page should not make protocol culture the first obstacle. It should show what the NIP lets a product do, where it fits, what it risks and which parts of Nostr depend on it.

Standards floor

Turn protocol detail into a useful next click.

NIPs become easier when they stop looking like a pile of numbers. Start with identity, events, publishing, wallets, relays, moderation or app data; then open the standard that explains the behavior.
